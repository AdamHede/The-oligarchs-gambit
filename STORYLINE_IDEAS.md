## Storyline Ideas

### 1. The Plutonium Incident
**Theme:** Nuclear security crisis meets personal profit

A scandal emerges that weapons-grade plutonium has gone missing from a poorly-secured facility. The oligarch must decide whether to cover it up, find it, or profit from selling it to interested parties.

**Key Events:**
- Initial discovery and cover-up decision
- International inspectors arrive
- Black market buyer offers
- Terrorist group makes contact
- Radiation poisoning cases emerge
- Recovery operation vs. profit maximization

**Potential Legacies:**
- "Nuclear Profiteer" (negative)
- "Crisis Manager" (positive if recovered)
- "International Pariah" (if sold)

---

### 2. The Oligarch Olympics
**Theme:** Prestige project that becomes a money pit

Hosting a major international sporting event. What starts as a propaganda victory becomes an endless corruption opportunity and potential diplomatic disaster.

**Key Events:**
- Initial bid and construction kickbacks
- Worker deaths on construction sites
- Massive cost overruns
- Terrorist threats
- Boycott movements
- Doping scandal
- Opening ceremony decisions
- Aftermath and white elephant stadiums

---

### 3. The Religious Revival
**Theme:** Orthodox Church gains power and influence

A charismatic patriarch emerges and the Church becomes a major political force. Navigate religious legitimacy vs. secular power.

**Key Events:**
- Patriarch offers blessing for legitimacy
- Church demands anti-LGBT laws
- Cathedral construction project
- Religious hard-liners vs. moderates
- Church wants control of education
- Patriarch accumulates wealth (rivalry?)
- Religious justification for war
- Excommunication threat

---

### 4. The Digital Ruble Gambit
**Theme:** Cryptocurrency meets authoritarian control

Launching a state cryptocurrency while running a private crypto scheme. Technology meets kleptocracy.

**Key Events:**
- Launch decision and profit skimming
- Western sanctions evasion via crypto
- Crypto mining energy crisis
- Digital ruble vs. private scheme
- Blockchain transparency problems
- Crypto crash wipes out citizens
- International money laundering exposure

---

### 5. The Succession War
**Theme:** Multiple factions fight for power as aging oligarch declines

Triggered after many years in power or at certain age/health events. Different protégés, generals, oligarchs, and family members compete.

**Key Events:**
- Health scare makes succession urgent
- Prime Minister builds own power base
- Defense Minister and FSB chief compete
- Oligarch's son wants power
- Regional governors rebel
- Forced "retirement" to ceremonial role
- Power-sharing compromise
- Successful transition vs. chaotic civil war

---

### 6. The Climate Disaster
**Theme:** Environmental catastrophe in oil-dependent state

Major environmental disaster (Siberian wildfires, permafrost melt, oil spill) forces decisions between climate adaptation, economic interests, and public health.

**Key Events:**
- Initial disaster and response
- Citizens demand climate action
- Oil industry threatens if restricted
- International climate pressure
- Refugee crisis from affected regions
- Opportunity to sell "green" credits while polluting
- Arctic oil rush vs. environmental protection

---

### 7. The Shadow War
**Theme:** Covert operations and plausible deniability

Series of events involving mercenaries, cyber attacks, assassinations abroad, and hybrid warfare. All deniable, all risky.

**Key Events:**
- Establish private military company
- Mercenaries deployed to foreign conflict
- Cyber attack on Western infrastructure
- Assassination on foreign soil
- Mercenaries captured/exposed
- Chemical weapons in foreign land
- Election interference operations
- Attribution and consequences

---

### 8. The Pandemic Response
**Theme:** Public health crisis meets authoritarianism

A serious pandemic emerges. Use it for control, profit from vaccines, suppress information, or actually help citizens.

**Key Events:**
- Initial outbreak and cover-up decision
- Lockdown vs. economy
- Vaccine nationalism and profiteering
- Fake cure promotion
- Whistleblower doctors
- Mass graves and denial
- International aid offers
- Economic collapse vs. health protection

## Recurring Event Ideas

### Economic Management
- Central Bank Independence - Fire competent banker vs. keep
- Tax Haven Crackdown - International pressure on offshore wealth
- Stock Market Crash - Bailout oligarchs or let them fail
- Brain Drain - Scientists/engineers leaving
- Import Substitution - Sanctions force domestic production
- Gold Reserves - Sell for cash or hold for stability
- Default Risk - Service foreign debt or default

### Social Control
- University Protests - Target intellectuals
- Labor Strike - Workers in key industry strike
- Draft Dodging - Sons of elite avoiding service
- Emigration Wave - Citizens fleeing country
- Social Media Outrage - Viral scandal, censorship decision
- Blogger Criticism - Popular blogger attacks you
- Historical Revisionism - Rewrite textbooks for propaganda

### International Relations
- Arms Deal Opportunity - Sell weapons to questionable regime
- Spy Exchange - Trade captured spies
- Diplomatic Incident - Ambassador insults foreign leader
- Border Provocation - Military incident with neighbor
- Trade War - Tariffs and counter-tariffs
- International Arrest Warrant - ICC wants you
- Extradition Request - Ally wants to extradite your critic

### Personal Power
- Mistress Scandal - Affair becomes public
- Family Corruption - Relative caught in scandal
- Birthday Celebration - Lavish party vs. modest image
- Official Residence - Another palace or show restraint
- Personal Security Paranoia - Increase security or seem weak
- Health Rumors - Address speculation or ignore

### Infrastructure & Prestige
- Bridge to Nowhere - Vanity infrastructure project
- High-Speed Rail - Massive investment, huge corruption
- New Capital City - Move capital to pet project city
- National Stadium - Sports prestige project
- Monument to Self - Literal statue building
- Library/Museum - Cultural prestige vs. cost

## Cross-Storyline Connections

### Sanctions + War
- War triggers sanctions automatically
- Sanctions severity depends on war crimes
- Sanctions can trigger economic collapse
- Economic collapse can trigger war as distraction

### Protests + Media Control
- Heavy censorship increases protest radicalization
- Protests create social media moments
- Brutal crackdown goes viral despite censorship

### Oligarch Rivalry + Succession
- Rivals position themselves as successors
- Eliminating rivals weakens succession options
- Family vs. oligarchs vs. military factions

### War + Regional Crisis
- Separatists emboldened by military being elsewhere
- Or: Use war to justify crushing separatists
- Conscription feeds both conflicts

### Economic Crisis + Everything
- Low treasury makes all decisions harder
- Can't buy loyalty, can't fund military, can't appease public
- Desperation drives extreme choices

## Rare Legacy Ideas

Reserve for truly significant moments:

- "Nuclear Power" - Acquired nuclear weapons
- "Empire Builder" - Successfully annexed multiple territories
- "Survived the Revolution" - Defeated major uprising
- "Immortal Leader" - Ruled 30+ years
- "Modernizer" - Actually improved country (very hard path)
- "The Bunker" - Ruled from hiding after coup attempt
- "Exile" - Fled to foreign country
- "The Trial" - International criminal court conviction
