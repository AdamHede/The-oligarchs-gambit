# Writing Guide

This guide covers the creative aspects of event creation: tone, style, balance, and narrative craft. For technical implementation details, see `EVENT_SYSTEM.md`.

---

## Core Game Philosophy

### The Central Tension

The game revolves around managing four competing pressures:

1. **Personal Enrichment** - Siphoning wealth from the state
2. **Elite Loyalty** - Keeping oligarchs, generals, and officials happy
3. **Public Compliance** - Preventing revolution through fear or appeasement
4. **State Functionality** - Maintaining enough treasury to prevent collapse

The optimal strategy is aggressive wealth extraction while keeping the system from imploding. Players should feel the constant tension between greed and survival.

### Player Position

The player is **complicit, not righteous**. They're not playing a hero trying to fix a broken system; they're playing an oligarch trying to maintain power and accumulate wealth.

- No "good guy" options - all choices have moral compromise
- Personal enrichment is always possible but comes with costs
- The game doesn't judge, but consequences speak for themselves
- Game over is inevitable - authoritarian power is unsustainable

## Tone

### Core Tone

- **Cynical but not nihilistic**: Shows corruption while maintaining engagement
- **Sardonic humor**: Laughing at the absurdity of power
- **Grounded in reality**: Inspired by real events without being preachy
- **Gritty and unvarnished**: No sugarcoating the moral compromises

### Narrative Voice

Third-person omniscient with a knowing, slightly ironic tone:

- ❌ "You are presented with a difficult choice."
- ✅ "Your generals present invasion plans. Intelligence says the operation will take 72 hours maximum."

- ❌ "The people are unhappy about corruption."
- ✅ "Your PR team is panicking - bread lines are getting longer and social media is exploding with anger."

### DO

- Be brutally realistic about authoritarian tactics
- Use dark satire to highlight absurdities of power
- Reference real historical patterns (without naming specific people)
- Show consequences of corruption clearly
- Make moral choices feel genuinely difficult
- Use specific, concrete details (amounts, methods, consequences)
- Include gallows humor from the perspective of cynical advisors

### DON'T

- Be gratuitously violent or gleeful about suffering
- Make light of real atrocities
- Be preachy or moralistic
- Sugarcoat the brutality of authoritarian rule
- Offer "good" choices (all should have moral compromise)
- Use direct country/person names (Russia, Putin, etc.)

## Writing Style

### Event Titles

**Format:** Short, punchy, evocative (2-6 words)

✅ Good:
- "The Yacht Incident"
- "An Inconvenient Reporter"
- "The Price of Bread"

❌ Bad:
- "An Oligarch Has a Party on His Yacht and People Are Mad"
- "Food Inflation Crisis Requires Response"

### Event Descriptions

**Format:** 2-4 sentences building tension

**Structure:**
1. Setup - What's happening
2. Complication - What makes this difficult
3. Stakes - What's at risk
4. Vivid detail - One specific thing that brings it to life

**Example:**
> "Your aluminum magnate friend just acquired a 500-foot superyacht with a submarine dock and missile defense system. He's hosting a party in the Mediterranean. Half your cabinet will be there with their mistresses. Your PR team is panicking - bread lines are getting longer and social media is exploding with anger."

### Choice Text

**Format:** 6-15 words, active voice, imperative mood

✅ Good:
- "Launch the invasion. Annex their resources."
- "Eliminate her. Make it look like a robbery gone wrong."
- "Arrest him. Seize everything. Send him to Siberia."

❌ Bad:
- "I think we should probably consider launching an invasion"
- "The elimination option"

### Numbers and Specificity

Be specific - numbers create verisimilitude and dark humor:

- "Your 23-year-old nephew just incorporated a 'consulting firm' last week"
- "Features include: aqua-disco, hookah lounge, underground ice hockey rink, casino, and an $850,000 Italian toilet"
- "50,000 people are in the streets"

## Satirical Techniques

### Juxtaposition

Place luxury and suffering side by side:

> "Total cost: $1.4 billion. The annual education budget is $800 million."

> "He's offering 40% of a $10 billion contract directly to your Cyprus account. His body armor fails ballistic tests."

### Euphemism and Doublespeak

Use the language of power:
- "Special military operation" (not "invasion")
- "Pacification efforts" (not "crackdown")
- "Consulting fees" (not "bribes")
- "Disappear" (not "kill")

But also **break the euphemism** when darkly funny:
> "Your FSB chief offers three options: novichok, a car accident, or we could just shoot her in the elevator."

### Understated Horror

Don't melodramatize - present dark content matter-of-factly:

> "Your GRU chief says they can 'handle it'—polonium, novichok, or a simple heart attack."

No exclamation points. No "evil laugh" moment. Just options, like ordering from a menu.

## Balance Guidelines

### Effect Scaling

#### Personal Wealth (Billions)
| Size | Amount | Examples |
|------|--------|----------|
| Tiny | ±1-2B | Skimming, minor bribes |
| Small | ±3-5B | Medium corruption, deals |
| Medium | ±6-10B | Major corruption, asset seizures |
| Large | ±11-20B | Massive deals, oligarch wealth |
| Extreme | ±20-50B | Once-in-game opportunities |

#### Treasury (Billions)
| Size | Amount | Examples |
|------|--------|----------|
| Tiny | ±10-30B | Minor programs |
| Small | ±40-80B | Medium programs, subsidies |
| Medium | ±90-150B | Major infrastructure |
| Large | ±160-250B | War, major economic policy |
| Extreme | ±260-400B | Sanctions, economic collapse |

#### Elite Approval (Percentage)
| Size | Amount | Examples |
|------|--------|----------|
| Tiny | ±2-5% | Minor favors/slights |
| Small | ±6-10% | Arrests, appointments |
| Medium | ±11-15% | Power sharing, major decisions |
| Large | ±16-25% | Purges, constitutional changes |

#### Public Anger (Percentage)
| Size | Amount | Examples |
|------|--------|----------|
| Tiny | ±2-5% | Minor scandals |
| Small | ±6-10% | Corruption revelations, price hikes |
| Medium | ±11-20% | Repression, visible wealth displays |
| Large | ±21-35% | Massacres, economic collapse |

### The Siphon Mechanic

Most events should offer ways to extract wealth at the cost of:
- Treasury depletion (directly taking from state)
- Elite anger (not sharing enough)
- Public anger (visible corruption)

### Typical Choice Pattern

1. **Greedy Choice**: High personal wealth gain, high costs elsewhere
2. **Balanced Choice**: Moderate gains/costs across metrics
3. **Prudent Choice**: Sacrifice personal gain for stability

## Legacy Achievements

### Weight Guidelines

| Type | Weight | Examples |
|------|--------|----------|
| Minor positive | +1 to +5 | Small symbolic wins |
| Moderate positive | +6 to +10 | Notable accomplishments |
| Major positive | +11 to +15 | Significant historical impact |
| Legendary positive | +16 to +20 | Defining characteristics |
| Minor scandal | -1 to -5 | Embarrassing moments |
| Moderate scandal | -6 to -10 | Serious reputation damage |
| Major atrocity | -11 to -15 | War crimes, mass repression |
| Historical infamy | -16 to -20 | Defining evil |

### Legacy Naming

- **Ironic**: "Democratic Champion (87%)"
- **Euphemistic**: "Liberator" (for invader)
- **Blunt**: "War Profiteer"
- **Darkly Humorous**: "Pirate King"

Format: 1-4 words maximum

### Legacy Icons

- ⚔️ Military/War
- 💰💸 Money/Corruption
- 🎭 Deception/Propaganda
- 👑 Power/Authority
- 💀☠️ Death/Destruction
- 🤐 Silence/Censorship
- ⚖️ Justice (or "Justice")
- 🔒 Control/Surveillance

## Common Event Patterns

### The Corruption Dilemma
Personal wealth gain vs. state treasury health
- Examples: Pipeline deals, defense contracts, privatization

### The Repression Choice
How brutal to be against opposition
- Examples: Protests, dissidents, free press

### The Elite Bargain
Keep oligarchs happy vs. maintain personal power
- Examples: Rival oligarchs, succession, power sharing

### The International Crisis
Short-term gain vs. long-term sanctions/isolation
- Examples: Invasions, assassinations abroad, resource weaponization

### The Spectacle Event
Expensive displays of power vs. treasury conservation
- Examples: Military parades, space programs, palaces

### The Economic Crisis
How to handle self-inflicted economic damage
- Examples: Inflation, bank failures, currency collapse

## Cultural References

### Be Oblique

Don't name:
- Specific countries (Russia, Ukraine)
- Specific people (Putin, Navalny)
- Specific companies (Gazprom, Rosneft)

Do reference:
- "The West"
- "A neighboring territory"
- "Ethnic minorities"
- Recognizable situations

This provides:
1. Legal safety (satire, not defamation)
2. Universal applicability (could be any autocrat)
3. Player complicity (harder to distance yourself)
4. Timelessness (won't date quickly)
