## New Storyline Ideas

### 1. The Plutonium Incident
**Theme:** Nuclear security crisis meets personal profit

**Concept:** A scandal emerges that weapons-grade plutonium has gone missing from a poorly-secured facility. The oligarch must decide whether to cover it up, find it, or profit from selling it to interested parties.

**Potential Events:**
- Initial discovery and cover-up decision
- International inspectors arrive
- Black market buyer offers
- Terrorist group makes contact
- Radiation poisoning cases emerge
- International ultimatum
- Recovery operation vs. profit maximization

**Legacy Outcomes:**
- "Nuclear Profiteer" (high negative legacy)
- "Crisis Manager" (positive if recovered)
- "International Pariah" (if sold to terrorists)

## Event Mechanic Ideas

### Decision Threshold Cascades
**Idea:** Events that track repeated choices and trigger increasingly severe consequences

Examples:
- Ignore worker safety 5 times → Major industrial disaster
- Assassinate opponents 3 times → International manhunt
- Rig elections repeatedly → Color revolution
- Ignore environmental violations → Catastrophic disaster

### Metric Combo Triggers
**Idea:** Events that only trigger when multiple metrics are in specific ranges

Examples:
- High wealth + low treasury + high anger = "Eat the Rich" uprising
- Low elite + high anger = Perfect storm for coup
- High treasury + low wealth = Slush fund temptation
- High elite + low anger = Consolidation opportunity

### Time Bomb Events
**Idea:** Events that add future consequences to the pool with delays

Examples:
- Steal pension fund → 5 years later, pension crisis
- Start war → 2-3 years of war events possible
- Constitutional change → Can't be undone, locks in legacy

### Reputation Tracker
**Idea:** Track international reputation separately, affects diplomacy events

Could affect:
- Sanctions likelihood
- Assassination difficulty abroad
- Olympic hosting possibilities
- EU/NATO expansion events

## Cross-Storyline Connection Ideas

### Sanctions + War
- War triggers sanctions automatically
- Sanctions severity depends on war crimes
- Sanctions can trigger economic collapse
- Economic collapse can trigger war as distraction

### Protests + Media Control
- Heavy censorship increases protest radicalization
- Protests create social media moments
- Brutal crackdown goes viral despite censorship

### Oligarch Rivalry + Succession
- Rivals position themselves as successors
- Eliminating rivals weakens succession options
- Family vs. oligarchs vs. military factions

### War + Regional Crisis
- Separatists emboldened by military being elsewhere
- Or: Use war to justify crushing separatists
- Conscription feeds both conflicts

### Economic Crisis + Everything
- Low treasury makes all decisions harder
- Can't buy loyalty, can't fund military, can't appease public
- Desperation drives extreme choices

## Legacy Achievement Ideas (Use Sparingly!)

### Should Be Reserved For:
- End of major storylines only
- True historical turning points
- Accumulation of many similar decisions (threshold-based)
- Game-changing constitutional moments
- Major wars, coups, or crises

### Potential Rare Legacy Ideas:
- "Nuclear Power" - Acquired nuclear weapons
- "Empire Builder" - Successfully annexed multiple territories
- "Survived the Revolution" - Defeated major uprising
- "Immortal Leader" - Ruled 30+ years
- "Modernizer" - Actually improved country (very hard path)
- "The Bunker" - Ruled from hiding after coup attempt
- "Exile" - Fled to foreign country
- "The Trial" - International criminal court conviction

## Mechanics to Avoid

### Don't Do:
- ❌ Too many random events with no connection
- ❌ Events that are just "good" or "bad" with no trade-off
- ❌ Events that require specific chains of 5+ prior events (too brittle)
- ❌ Events that make the game unwinnable early
- ❌ Pop culture references that break immersion
- ❌ Events that are too similar to existing ones
- ❌ Legacy achievements for minor decisions

### Do Instead:
- ✓ Events that create interesting dilemmas
- ✓ Events that can trigger in multiple ways
- ✓ Consequences that emerge organically from decisions
- ✓ Grounded, realistic scenarios
- ✓ Varied events within themes
- ✓ Legacy for truly significant moments
