# Oligarch Rivalry

## Theme & Tone
A "Game of Thrones" with superyachts and novichok. The tone is cynical, paranoid, and lavishly corrupt. It explores the delicate balancing act of keeping the elite "wolves" fed without letting them eat you—or each other (too loudly).

## Key Characters
- **The Energy Baron**: Head of the state oil/gas giant. Ruthless, old guard, demanding. Wants state assets for free.
- **The Tech Magnate**: Younger, "western-leaning" (owns a villa in Italy), runs the digital surveillance state.
- **The Aluminum King**: A classic gangster-turned-businessman. Crude but loyal if paid.
- **The Banker**: Runs the state bank. A technocrat trying to manage the chaos of the others' greed.

## Story Beats

1.  **Inciting Incident**: Two major oligarchs clash over a lucrative asset (e.g., a seized foreign company or a new state contract). They demand you pick a side.

2.  **Escalation**:
    -   **The Wedding War**: Competitive displays of wealth at their children's weddings.
    -   **The Private Army**: One oligarch starts hiring "security" that looks a lot like a militia.
    -   **The Leak**: Kompromat (compromising material) on one oligarch hits the press. Was it you, or his rival?

3.  **Complications**:
    -   **Asset Seizure**: Western sanctions hit one oligarch hard; he demands compensation from the state budget.
    -   **The "Accident"**: A minor oligarch falls out of a window. Everyone looks to you.
    -   **The Buyout**: An oligarch tries to buy political influence in the Duma/Parliament directly, bypassing you.

4.  **Climax**:
    -   **The Raid**: The FSB arrests a top oligarch on your orders (or without them?).
    -   **OR The Summit**: You host a "peace talks" dinner on your yacht where the main course is an ultimatum.

5.  **Resolution**:
    -   **Purge**: You jail/kill one faction, consolidating power but terrifying the rest (Loyalty drop).
    -   **Balance**: You divide the spoils, making everyone equally unhappy but rich (Wealth cost).
    -   **Puppet Master**: You play them against each other indefinitely, profiting from the chaos (High risk).

## Branching Points

-   **Back the Siloviki (Security Services) or the Businessmen?**
    -   Siloviki: Increases control, decreases economic efficiency.
    -   Businessmen: Increases wealth/economy, decreases your absolute control.

-   **Handle the "Disobedient" Oligarch:**
    -   *Arrest him*: Public show of strength. Assets seized (Treasury ++). Elite Loyalty --.
    -   *Negotiate*: Quiet deal. He keeps his assets, you get a cut. Elite Loyalty =.
    -   *Eliminate*: "Heart attack". Very effective, very scary.

-   **The Sanctions Bailout:**
    -   *Bail them out*: Use state funds to cover their yacht losses. Elite Loyalty ++, Treasury --.
    -   *Let them rot*: "Patriotism requires sacrifice." Elite Loyalty --, Public Compliance +.
