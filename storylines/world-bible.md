# World Bible: The Oligarchy

## Player Role & Game Philosophy

**You are the Oligarch.** Not just any oligarch—you're the one who clawed your way to the very top. Through cunning, ruthlessness, and probably a few convenient "accidents," you now sit at the apex of power in The Motherland.

**Your Goals:**
- **Get Rich**: Accumulate obscene personal wealth. Billions aren't enough—you want your name on Forbes lists and palaces that make Versailles look quaint.
- **Stay in Power**: The throne is slippery. Keep the elites loyal, the public compliant, and your enemies six feet under (or in Siberia).
- **Write History**: Shape your legacy. Liberator? Modernizer? Strongman? The narrative is yours to craft.
- **Don't Lose Face**: Respect and fear are everything. Humiliation is worse than bankruptcy.

**The Tone**: This is dark comedy satire—cynical, absurd, and unapologetically corrupt. Think *Dr. Strangelove* meets *The Death of Stalin*. You're not here to be liked, make friends, or save the world. You're here to extract wealth, consolidate power, and occasionally make spectacularly terrible decisions that somehow work out (or don't).

**Gameplay Philosophy**: The game rewards bold, decisive action—usually of the questionable variety. Seize that yacht. Rig that election. Polonium that blogger. The "safe" choice often leads to slow decline, while the audacious choice opens new (terrifying) opportunities. This is an evil dictator simulator. Lean into it.

## The Setting: The Motherland (Rossiya-adjacent)

The game takes place in a vast, resource-rich, transcontinental nation simply called **"The Motherland"** (or sometimes just "The Federation"). It is a place of stark contrasts: frozen tundras sitting atop trillions in gas reserves, crumbling Soviet-era brutalist apartment blocks overshadowed by glittering neo-futurist skyscrapers, and babushkas selling pickles outside luxury boutiques that sell $5,000 handbags.

It is a parody of post-Soviet Russia, but with the dial turned up to 11. It captures the specific aesthetic of "petro-state authoritarianism":
-   **The Capital**: A city of ring roads, golden domes, and terrible traffic. The center is the **Kremlin** (or "The Citadel"), a fortress of red brick and paranoia.
-   **The Dacha Belt**: Where the elite live behind 12-foot fences, surrounded by birch forests and private security details.
-   **The Regions**: Everything else. A mix of industrial rust belts, vast agricultural plains, and resource colonies where the roads dissolve into mud every spring ("Rasputitsa").

## The Political System: "Managed Democracy"

The country is nominally a democracy. There is a Parliament (The Duma), a Constitution, and elections. However, everyone knows these are theater.

-   **The Social Contract**: The people stay out of politics, and in exchange, the state provides stability and modest prosperity (or at least, not chaos like the "The 90s").
-   **The Vertical of Power**: You sit at the top. Below you are the "Towers of the Kremlin"—warring factions of elites who constantly jockey for your favor. You are the arbiter, the only thing keeping them from eating each other.
-   **The Ideology**: A shapeshifting mix of nostalgia for the "Great Empire" of the past, conservative "Traditional Values," and cynical anti-Western sentiment. It doesn't have to make sense; it just has to feel good.

## The Factions (The "Towers")

1.  **The Siloviki (The Enforcers)**
    *   **Who**: Generals, FSB/KGB agents, Police Chiefs.
    *   **Vibe**: Grey suits, bad haircuts, unsmiling. They believe in force, order, and paranoia. They see "Western Spies" everywhere.
    *   **Want**: More budget for security, war, isolationism.
    *   **Key Figure**: *The Director* (Head of Secret Police).

2.  **The Oligarchs (The Wallet)**
    *   **Who**: Oil barons, metal magnates, bankers.
    *   **Vibe**: Italian suits, Swiss watches, London real estate. They are ruthlessly competent at stealing money but depend on the state for protection.
    *   **Want**: Open borders (for their money), stability, government contracts.
    *   **Key Figure**: *The Energy Baron* (CEO of Gaz-State).

3.  **The Technocrats (The Brains)**
    *   **Who**: The Central Banker, the Prime Minister, the Digital Minister.
    *   **Vibe**: Glasses, iPads, tired eyes. They are actually trying to make the country work despite the corruption. They are complicit but see themselves as "saving the country from chaos."
    *   **Want**: Economic stability, modernization, digitalization.
    *   **Key Figure**: *The Governor* (Head of the Central Bank).

4.  **The Patriots (The Screamers)**
    *   **Who**: TV Propagandists, "Z-Bloggers," The Church.
    *   **Vibe**: Loud, aggressive, red-faced. They demand blood and glory on talk shows while sending their kids to university in Paris.
    *   **Want**: Total war, cultural purges, monuments.
    *   **Key Figure**: *The Anchor* (TV Host).

## The Mood

The overwhelming feeling is **Cynicism**.
-   Corruption is not a bug; it's the operating system.
-   Truth is not objective; it's whatever the TV says today.
-   Safety is an illusion; you can be a billionaire today and in a penal colony tomorrow.
