# Succession Crisis

## Theme & Tone
Shakespearean tragedy meets geriatric ward. The tone is anxious and inevitable. It deals with the frailty of the physical body versus the absolute nature of the political position. Everyone is smiling, everyone is plotting.

## Key Characters
-   **The Leader (You)**: Aging, paranoid, potentially sick.
-   **The Dauphin (The Son/Daughter)**: Entitled, incompetent, but "blood".
-   **The Spy Chief**: Knows where all the bodies are buried. Wants the throne or to be the power behind it.
-   **The Bodyguard**: The former head of security who rose too high. Fiercely loyal, or is he?

## Story Beats

1.  **Inciting Incident**: A health scare. You collapse during a televised speech, or vanish for a week for "medical treatment," sparking rumors.

2.  **Escalation**:
    -   **The Rumor Mill**: Telegram channels buzz with "insider info" about your death.
    -   **The Jockeying**: Ministers start announcing policies without your approval.
    -   **The Grooming**: You try to give the Dauphin a real job (Head of a State Corp). They mess it up spectacularly.

3.  **Complications**:
    -   **The Diagnosis**: A doctor gives you bad news. Do you trust the doctor?
    -   **The Betrayal**: A trusted advisor is caught meeting with foreign diplomats or rivals.
    -   **The Constitutional Change**: Changing the law to allow you to rule forever or appoint a successor.

4.  **Climax**:
    -   **The Surgery**: You must go under anesthesia. Who holds the nuclear briefcase?
    -   **OR The Coup Attempt**: Someone tries to accelerate the process.

5.  **Resolution**:
    -   **Dynasty**: You successfully install your child. (Stability ?, Competence --)
    -   **Eternal Leader**: You recover, purge the doubters, and rule "forever". (Fear ++)
    -   **Puppet**: You are technically in charge, but the Spy Chief pulls the strings.
    -   **Darkest Timeline**: Civil war creates a power vacuum.

## Branching Points

-   **Announce a Successor?**
    -   *Yes*: Stabilizes the system but makes you a "lame duck".
    -   *No*: Keeps you powerful but risks chaos if you die suddenly.

-   **Trust the Family or the System?**
    -   *Family*: Corrupt, incompetent, but loyal blood.
    -   *System (Siloviki/Technocrats)*: Competent, ruthless, might kill you to take your spot.

-   **Handle the Health Leak:**
    -   *Deny everything*: "The President's handshake is strong."
    -   *Show strength*: Stage a judo match or hockey game (using body doubles if needed).
    -   *Purge the doctors*: Silence the source.
