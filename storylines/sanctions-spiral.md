# Sanctions Spiral

## Theme & Tone
A slow-motion economic train wreck. The tone is bureaucratic desperation masked by defiant propaganda. It's about the absurdity of "Import Substitution" (replacing French cheese with local rubber) and the gritty reality of a shrinking world.

## Key Characters
-   **The Central Banker**: Highly competent, secretly horrified, trying to keep the Titanic floating.
-   **The Propagandist**: Spins economic collapse as "sovereignty" and "strength".
-   **The Smuggler**: The new hero of the economy, bringing in iPhones and airplane parts via Kazakhstan.
-   **The Western Envoy**: The voice of the tightening noose.

## Story Beats

1.  **Inciting Incident**: A major geopolitical move (War/Annexation) triggers a wave of crushing sanctions.

2.  **Escalation**:
    -   **The Freeze**: Assets abroad are frozen. No more shopping in Milan.
    -   **The Exodus**: Western brands leave. IKEA is replaced by "Idea". McDonald's by "Uncle Vanya's".
    -   **The Ruble Crash**: Currency freefall. Panic at ATMs.

3.  **Complications**:
    -   **Tech Starvation**: Planes stop flying due to lack of parts. Servers crash.
    -   **The Brain Drain**: IT specialists flee to Georgia/Armenia/Dubai.
    -   **Energy Embargo**: You can't sell your oil/gas to the usual buyers. Discount sales to the East.

4.  **Climax**:
    -   **The Default**: The state cannot pay its foreign debts.
    -   **OR The Autarky**: Complete economic isolation. North Korea model.

5.  **Resolution**:
    -   **Fortress Economy**: You survive, poorer but independent. (Stability +, Wealth --)
    -   **Client State**: You become economically dependent on a larger eastern neighbor (China).
    -   **Collapse**: Hyperinflation destroys the regime.

## Branching Points

-   **Response to Brand Exodus:**
    -   *Nationalize them*: Seize the factories. (Short term gain, long term inefficiency).
    -   *Let them go*: Respect property rights to hope they return? (Weakness).

-   **The Central Bank Policy:**
    -   *Technocratic*: High interest rates, painful austerity. Saves the economy, angers the people.
    -   *Populist*: Print money! Fix prices! (Leads to shortages and hyperinflation).

-   **Pivot to the East:**
    -   *Sell cheap*: Sell resources at massive discounts to new partners.
    -   *Hold out*: Refuse to sell below market rates (Budget collapse).
