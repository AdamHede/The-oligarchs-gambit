# The Cultural Purge ("The War on Woke")

## Theme & Tone
Absurdist tragedy. The tone is hysterical and petty. It highlights the ridiculousness of a nuclear superpower worrying about what a rapper is wearing or what a theater director said in 2014. It’s about "Traditional Values" as a weapon of mass distraction.

## Key Characters
-   **The Censor**: Head of the "Safe Internet League." A zealot who scours Instagram for nipples and "disrespect."
-   **The Pop Star**: The "Madonna" of the Motherland. Aging, wealthy, and formerly loyal, but she just posted a black square on Instagram.
-   **The Director**: A celebrated filmmaker who makes movies about how sad and cold the country is (which wins awards in Cannes but angers the Patriots).

## Story Beats

1.  **Inciting Incident**: **"The Almost-Naked Party."** An eccentric blogger hosts a party where the dress code is "almost naked." The entire elite attends—pop stars, TV hosts, even your goddaughter. Photos leak during a bad week at the front.

2.  **Escalation**:
    -   **The Outrage**: The "Patriots" on Telegram go berserk. "Our boys are dying while they show their asses!"
    -   **The Apology Tour**: You force the stars to film tearful apologies in modest turtlenecks.
    -   **The Cancellations**: Concerts are cancelled. Faces are blurred out on TV.

3.  **Complications**:
    -   **The Blacklists**: The Censor presents a list of "unreliable" artists to be banned from radio/streaming. It includes 80% of the top charts.
    -   **The Brain Drain**: The creative class starts leaving for Berlin and Tel Aviv. The only artists left are 70-year-old accordion players.
    -   **The Rap Battle**: A popular young rapper writes a diss track about the Censor. It goes viral among the youth.

4.  **Climax**:
    -   **The Show Trial**: A theater director is arrested for "justifying terrorism" in a play about the 17th century.
    -   **OR The Eurovision Alternative**: You launch a "Patriotic Song Contest" to replace Western culture. It is cringe-inducingly bad.

5.  **Resolution**:
    -   **Cultural Desert**: You crush the liberals. Culture is now only folk dancing and war songs. (Patriots ++, Happiness --).
    -   **Selective Mercy**: You protect the famous ones but crush the small ones. (Cynicism ++).
    -   **Underground Revival**: Banning them makes them cooler. Samizdat (illegal art) returns.

## Branching Points

-   **Handle the "Naked Party":**
    -   *Ignore it*: "Let them have fun." (Patriots Anger ++).
    -   *Public Shaming*: Force apologies. (Elite Fear ++).
    -   *Jail Time*: Make an example. (International mockery).

-   **The "Foreign Agent" Law:**
    -   *Expand it*: Anyone with a YouTube channel is a spy.
    -   *Limit it*: Only real political threats.
