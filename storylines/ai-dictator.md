# The AI Dictator ("Cyber-Gulag")

## Theme & Tone
Black Mirror meets The Office. High-tech dystopia managed by low-tech bureaucrats. The tone is cold, efficient, yet glitchy. It explores the horror of being watched by a machine that doesn't quite understand humanity (or corruption).

## Key Characters
-   **The Digital Minister**: A young technocrat who thinks code can solve fascism.
-   **The Algorithm**: Not a person, but a presence. It flags "disloyalty" based on web history.
-   **The General**: Doesn't understand "the cloud" but wants a "red button" to turn off the internet.

## Story Beats

1.  **Inciting Incident**: **Implementation of "Face Pay" and Digital IDs.** The subway system now requires a face scan. It's convenient, but the data goes straight to the FSB.

2.  **Escalation**:
    -   **The Camera Network**: Installing 100,000 cameras with facial recognition.
    -   **The Leak**: A database of everyone's food delivery orders leaks. We now know the Secret Police Chief loves American pizza.
    -   **The Digital Draft**: Conscription notices are now sent digitally. Deleting the app is a crime.

3.  **Complications**:
    -   **The Glitch**: The system accidentally flags loyalists as terrorists because of a coding error.
    -   **The Deepfake**: A video of YOU announcing your resignation appears. It looks 99% real.
    -   **The Firewall**: You try to disconnect from the global internet ("Cheburashka Net"). The banking system crashes immediately.

4.  **Climax**:
    -   **The Social Credit Score**: Rolling out a system where loyalty = access to money/travel.
    -   **OR The Blackout**: Hackers (or the West) delete the tax database.

5.  **Resolution**:
    -   **Digital Panopticon**: Total surveillance. Crime is down, but so is joy. (Control ++, Innovation --).
    -   **Paper Reversion**: The tech fails too much. You go back to stamps and paper files. (Inefficiency ++).
    -   **Cyber-Feudalism**: Tech exists, but only the rich can buy privacy.

## Branching Points

-   **Handle the Deepfake:**
    -   *Deny*: "It's fake!" (But now people doubt real videos).
    -   *Ban AI*: Make the tech illegal. (Stops progress).
    -   *Create your own*: Flooding the zone with noise.

-   **The Sovereign Internet Test:**
    -   *Pull the plug*: Cut the cables. (Economic damage severe).
    -   *Soft block*: Just slow down Twitter and YouTube.
