# The Religious Revival

## Theme & Tone
Censers, gold icons, and nuclear missiles. The tone is archaic yet strictly modern political technology. It explores the cynical use of faith to bolster legitimacy and the dangerous game of empowering religious zealots.

## Key Characters
-   **The Patriarch**: An ex-KGB agent in golden robes. Loyal to the state, but loves luxury.
-   **The Zealot Oligarch**: Funds private Christian militias and TV channels. More Catholic than the Pope (or Orthodox than the Patriarch).
-   **The Liberal Bishop**: A rare voice of conscience (and a target).
-   **The Cultural Minister**: Enforces "Traditional Values".

## Story Beats

1.  **Inciting Incident**: Needing a boost in legitimacy, you turn to the Church for a public blessing or to condemn your enemies.

2.  **Escalation**:
    -   **The Cathedral of War**: Building a massive military cathedral with mosaics of guns and angels.
    -   **The Values Law**: Banning "non-traditional relationships" to distract from economic woes.
    -   **The Holy Water**: Priests blessing intercontinental ballistic missiles.

3.  **Complications**:
    -   **The Zealots**: The religious right demands you ban abortion or evolution, alienating the educated urban class.
    -   **The Scandal**: The Patriarch's luxury watch is photoshopped out, but the reflection remains.
    -   **The Schism**: Churches in neighboring countries break away from your Patriarchate.

4.  **Climax**:
    -   **The Crusade**: The Church fully endorses a war as a "Holy War" against Satanism.
    -   **OR The Inquisition**: A witch hunt against "foreign agents" and "traitors" within the culture.

5.  **Resolution**:
    -   **God-Emperor**: You are the defender of the faith. (Legitimacy with poor/rural ++, Educated flight ++).
    -   **Secular Break**: You curb the Church's power before it overtakes you. (Patriarch anger --).
    -   **Theocracy Lite**: The state and church merge completely.

## Branching Points

-   **Funding the Church:**
    -   *Lavish*: Gold domes everywhere. (Patriarch Loyalty ++, Treasury --).
    -   *Modest*: Keep them hungry.

-   **Traditional Values Laws:**
    -   *Strict*: Ban everything western/liberal. (Public Compliance (Rural) +, Brain Drain ++).
    -   *Moderate*: Lip service only.

-   **The Patriarch's Scandal:**
    -   *Cover it up*: Use FSB to silence critics.
    -   *Let him sweat*: Remind him who is boss.
