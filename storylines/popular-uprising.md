# Popular Uprising

## Theme & Tone
The rumble of the street. The tone shifts from dismissive arrogance to genuine fear. It explores the friction between the "TV" (propaganda) and the "Fridge" (economic reality).

## Key Characters
-   **The Blogger**: Charismatic, internet-savvy opposition leader. Investigates your corruption.
-   **The Police Chief**: A brute who believes a baton solves every problem.
-   **The Spin Doctor**: Wants to manage protests with fake opposition parties and distractions.
-   **The Student**: Representative of the younger generation who hates you.

## Story Beats

1.  **Inciting Incident**: A trigger event—a rigged election, a massive corruption expose (The Palace), or an unpopular law (raising pension age).

2.  **Escalation**:
    -   **The Viral Video**: "The Golden Toilet Brush" investigation gets 100M views.
    -   **The First Rally**: Unsunctioned protests in the capital. Schoolchildren and hipsters.
    -   **The Crackdown**: Police beat protesters. Images go viral, increasing anger.

3.  **Complications**:
    -   **The Strike**: Factory workers join the hipsters. Now it's dangerous.
    -   **The Defection**: A minor police unit refuses to beat grandmothers.
    -   **The Martyr**: A protest leader dies in custody.

4.  **Climax**:
    -   **The Maidan Moment**: Protesters occupy the central square. Barricades go up.
    -   **The Order**: You must decide whether to order the army to shoot.

5.  **Resolution**:
    -   **Tiananmen**: You crush the movement with brutal force. (Fear ++, Legitimacy --, Sanctions ++).
    -   **Concession**: You sacrifice a Prime Minister or repeal a law. (Weakness ?, Stability +).
    -   **Flight**: You flee to a friendly dictatorship. Game Over.

## Branching Points

-   **Handle the Blogger:**
    -   *Ignore*: Hope he fades away.
    -   *Jail*: Turn him into a symbol.
    -   *Poison*: The "active measure". High risk of exposure.

-   **Response to Protests:**
    -   *Propaganda*: "They are paid by the West/State Department."
    -   *Police*: Arrests and fines.
    -   *National Guard*: Beatings and tear gas.
    -   *Army*: Live ammunition.

-   **Internet Control:**
    -   *Slowdown*: Throttle Twitter/YouTube.
    -   *Blackout*: Turn off the internet. (Destroys economy).
    -   *Sovereign Net*: Cut off from the global web completely.
