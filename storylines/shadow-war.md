# The Shadow War

## Theme & Tone
Cold War paranoia meets modern hybrid warfare. The tone is whispered, secretive, and menacing. It's about things that "didn't happen" but definitely did. The player operates in the grey zone where the line between state policy, organized crime, and terrorism blurs.

## Key Characters
-   **The GRU Chief**: A man who speaks in riddles and loves cathedral spires. In charge of military intelligence and "wet work".
-   **The Cyber Commander**: A hoodie-wearing genius who runs troll farms and DDoS attacks.
-   **The Private Contractor**: Not officially military, but fights your wars. Deniable asset.
-   **The Foreign Minister**: Has to lie to the UN about everything you do.

## Story Beats

1.  **Inciting Incident**: A defector or critic abroad is becoming too loud. The decision to silence them starts a chain of covert operations.

2.  **Escalation**:
    -   **The Cyber Strike**: Taking down a rival nation's power grid or banking system.
    -   **The Election Meddling**: Funding fringe parties in enemy nations.
    -   **The Mercenary Deployment**: Sending "instructors" to an African resource-rich nation.

3.  **Complications**:
    -   **The Botched Job**: An assassination attempt fails, leaving evidence (passports, receipts) behind.
    -   **The Exposure**: Bellingcat or foreign intel reveals the identities of your agents.
    -   **The Blowback**: A cyberweapon you unleashed comes back to hit your own infrastructure.

4.  **Climax**:
    -   **The Ultimatum**: Western leaders present undeniable proof and threaten massive retaliation.
    -   **OR The False Flag**: Staging a terrorist attack to justify open war.

5.  **Resolution**:
    -   **Master of Shadows**: You are feared globally. No one knows what is true. (Intimidation ++, Trust --).
    -   **Rogue State**: You are labeled a state sponsor of terrorism. (Sanctions ++).
    -   **Plausible Deniability**: You sacrifice your agents to save face. "We never heard of them."

## Branching Points

-   **Method of Elimination:**
    -   *The Umbrella*: High-tech poison. Sends a message.
    -   *The Accident*: Car crash or window fall. Deniable.
    -   *The Bullet*: Crude but effective.

-   **Response to Exposure:**
    -   *Deny*: "Russophobia! Nonsense!"
    -   *Troll*: Mock the accusers. "If we wanted to kill him, he'd be dead."
    -   *Admit (Privately)*: Trade the captured agents.

-   **Use of Mercenaries:**
    -   *Full Support*: Give them heavy weapons. (Effectiveness ++, Deniability --).
    -   *Cut Them Loose*: If they get in trouble, they are on their own. (Loyalty --).
